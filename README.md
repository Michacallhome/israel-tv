# ערוצי ישראל / Israel Live TV

A single-page web app that plays Israeli TV channels which stream publicly outside Israel.
Built for TV browsers (remote-friendly tiles, number keys, channel up/down) and phones
(installs to the iPhone home screen, native player, AirPlay).

Channels play from the public web feeds published by the channels themselves or their
streaming providers. Keshet 12, Sport 5 and the yes/HOT channels are geo-blocked
outside Israel and are not included.

Hebrew: Kan 11, Reshet 13, Now 14, Kan Hinuchit, i24NEWS Hebrew, Channel 16, Knesset
Channel, Hidabroot, Kabbalah for the People, and Reshet 13's Reality, Comedy and
Vacation channels. Other languages: Channel 9 (Russian), i24NEWS English, French and
Arabic, Makan 33 (Arabic), Hala TV (Arabic), Israel Pars TV (Persian).

Files: `index.html` (the whole app), `manifest.webmanifest`, icons.
